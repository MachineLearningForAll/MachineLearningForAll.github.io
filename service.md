Academic service reflects the trust universities and professional communities place in
researchers to evaluate work fairly, maintain standards, and support the next generation.
My roles include leading a professional society chapter, examining doctoral theses at other
universities, and committee work at Aalto. My independent volunteer work against academic
bullying has its own [Activism]({{ site.baseurl }}/activism) page.

### Professional societies

- **Chair of the IEEE Finland Section SP01/CAS04 Society Chapter, 2019–2023.**
  The joint chapter of the Signal Processing and Circuits and Systems Societies is the local home of the two societies in Finland: it brings researchers from the Finnish universities and industry together around invited talks and events, and acts as the link between them and the societies' international activities.
  Among other things, the chapter ran the annual IEEE Finland SP/CAS Paper Award during my term.
  IEEE recognised the term with a certificate of appreciation for *"Notable Services and Contributions towards the advancement of IEEE and the Engineering Professions."*

### University committees

- Member of the **Teaching Evaluation Committee (SCITEC)** of the Aalto University School of Science since 2023, also referred to as the school's Teaching Competence Assessment Committee.
  SCITEC assesses the teaching competence of candidates in tenure-track recruitment and in the later evaluations leading to tenured and promoted positions.
  It has an independent role: it issues its own statement on each candidate's teaching competence, along with recommendations such as pedagogical training or trying out different teaching methods.
  Members are drawn from across the school's departments and include an educational developer and students trained to evaluate teaching.
  I use the role to keep student well-being on the agenda alongside the usual metrics.
