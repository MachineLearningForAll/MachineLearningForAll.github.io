Trustworthy machine learning, explained clearly.

Welcome! I am Alexander Jung, a tenured Associate Professor of Machine Learning at Aalto University, Finland. I lead the Machine Learning for Big Data group. I develop mathematical foundations and practical methods for trustworthy and federated machine learning—and make these ideas accessible through teaching, books, supervision, and public talks.
